# Updating your website

A short guide to changing the words, pictures, art and music on Nothing Wrong With You.

---

## Logging in

1. Go to **nothingwrongwithyou.org/admin**.
2. Type your email and password and press **Log in**.

You'll see your **home screen**. It has big buttons for the things you'll do most:

- **Edit a page:** Home, About, Resources, Contact and the Art pages.
- **Add your art:** a collage piece, a song or a video.
- **The whole site:** settings and support messages, and **View your website**.

You can always get back to the home screen by clicking the star at the top left.

---

## Changing the words on a page

1. On your home screen, click the page you want, for example **Edit the Home page**.
2. The page opens in two halves. **On the left are the words you can change.** On the right is a **preview** of how the page will look.
3. Some pages are split into tabs along the top (for example *Top of page*, *Message*, *About Becca*). Click a tab to see that part of the page.
4. Click into any box and type.

**Your work saves itself as you type.** The preview on the right updates within a moment. Nobody else can see these changes yet.

When you're happy, press **Publish changes** (top right). Your website updates within a few seconds.

> **Tip:** The small grey text under each box explains what it's for and where it appears on the site.

---

## Longer writing (About, Resources, Contact)

Longer text has a small toolbar at the top:

- **B** makes text bold and ***I*** makes it italic.
- The heading menu turns a line into a heading.
- The link button makes a link. Select some words first, then click it and paste the web address. Links to other websites open in a new tab automatically.
- There are buttons for bullet lists, numbered lists, quotes and a dividing line.

You can also select some words to get a small floating toolbar with the same options.

---

## Replacing a picture

1. Open the page, and the tab that has the picture.
2. Next to the picture, click **Remove**.
3. Click **Add new** and choose a photo from your computer, or **Choose from library** to reuse one you've uploaded before.
4. **Describe the image** in one sentence, for example *"Becca smiling in a black rose-print top."* This is read aloud to people who can't see the picture, so it matters.
5. Press **Save**, then **Publish changes** on the page.

**Picture tips**
- JPG, PNG and WebP photos all work.
- Photos straight from a phone are fine, but very large files (over about 10 MB) make the page slower. If you can, send a smaller copy (about 2000 pixels wide).
- **Images** in the menu is your picture library. Changing the file of a picture *there* changes it everywhere it's used. To change a picture on just one page, upload a new one from that page instead.

## Adding a collage piece

1. On your home screen, click **Add a collage piece**.
2. Choose the image file.
3. **Describe the image** (what's in it, in a sentence).
4. Optionally add a title and year. They help you find pieces later.
5. Press **Publish changes**.

**Changing the order:** click **See all pieces or reorder**, then drag a piece by the handle on its left. The gallery shows them in the same order.

**Hiding a piece for now:** open it and choose **Unpublish** from the **⋯** menu. It disappears from the website but stays here, and you can publish it again later.

**Removing a piece for good:** open it, then choose **Delete** from the **⋯** menu at the top right.

---

## Adding a song

1. Click **Add a song**.
2. Choose the MP3 file.
3. Type the song title, and the artist (include anyone you worked with).
4. Optionally type the length, for example `4:11`.
5. Press **Publish changes**.

---

## Adding a video

1. Upload the video to YouTube first.
2. Click **Add a video**.
3. Paste the YouTube link. You can copy it from the **Share** button on YouTube.
4. Add a title and a line about the video.
5. Optionally add a cover image. If you don't, YouTube's own picture is used.
6. Press **Publish changes**.

The first video in the list is shown large. Drag to change the order.

---

## Previewing before you publish

- Pages show the preview beside the editor automatically.
- To see the page at phone size, use the size menu above the preview.
- To hide the preview and get more room to type, click the **eye** icon at the top.
- The preview shows a small **Preview mode** label in the bottom-right corner. Real visitors never see it.

---

## If you make a mistake

- **Not published yet?** Click the **⋯** menu next to **Publish changes** and choose **Discard unpublished changes**. The page goes back to what's live.
- **Already published?** Open the page and click **History**. Pick an earlier version and click **Bring back this version**, then publish.

Nothing you do in the editor can break the design of the site. Layout, colours and fonts are built in.

---

## Settings and support messages

**Site settings** (from your home screen) has four tabs:

- **General:** the site name, tagline, a summary for Google, and the picture shown when someone shares a link.
- **Social links:** the links in the Social menu, the footer and the Contact page. Drag to reorder.
- **Support messages:** the content note, crisis phone lines, legal notice and the quick exit button. **Untick a box to hide that message everywhere on the site.**
- **Contact form:** the Formspree code that makes the Contact form send messages to your email.

Settings go live as soon as you press **Save**. They don't need publishing.

---

## Your account

Click your picture (top right) to change your name, email or password. If you forget your password, ask your developer to reset it.

For safety, after 5 wrong password attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes.

---

## Getting help

If something looks wrong on the website after you publish, open **History** on that page and bring back the last good version. Then get in touch with your developer and tell them which page and roughly what time.
