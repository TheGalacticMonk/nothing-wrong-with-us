I'm Becca Berry, Esq., and I'm done pretending healing is pretty.

I'm a survivor. A truth-teller. A California-raised attorney who realized the law could sometimes protect people but couldn't save them, so I learned about the magic that could. I write, sing, collage, and conjure because my healing demanded it. I practice hoodoo because my ancestors showed me the way out. I create art because silence was killing me.

I'm multi-dimensional, which is just another way of saying I contain multitudes and I'm done apologizing for it.

I'm here as a trauma-informed legal advocate, spiritual guide, and unflinching witness to whatever process you're ready to pursue. The core value of my work is radical truth wrapped in unconditional love…because you can't heal what you won't name.

Nothing Wrong With You is the spiritual and artistic platform I've been building since 2017 for survivors who are tired of being told to heal quietly.

This work exists to end the gaslighting. To name the targeting. To speak the truth about trauma-based control systems that convince beautiful, powerful souls they're broken when really, they're just awake in a world designed to keep them asleep.

The trauma of abuse—familial, institutional, governmental, spiritual—will make you believe "there must be something wrong with me." Why am I depressed? Why can't I fit here? Why does this feel like a cage?

Because it is.

We're living in systems designed to extract our energy, our creativity, our power. There's nothing wrong with you. There never was. You're not too sensitive. You're not crazy. You're not the problem.

**You're the threat to their control.**

And now you get to reclaim every piece of yourself they tried to take. On your terms. With the law protecting your rights, the spirits protecting your path, and the art protecting your truth.

## My Spiritual Path

I was raised Christian and genuinely believed. Then my healing journey took me beyond religion into direct relationship with the Spirit of Truth, my ancestors, and the parts of spiritual practice the church called dangerous because they couldn't control it.

I practice left-hand and right-hand path work. I am a hoodoo. I work with the dark feminine. I communicate with my parts. I've learned that what they called rebellion was actually remembering, and what they called blasphemy was actually freedom.

My work combines my lived experience as a survivor with my training as an attorney and my calling as an artist and spiritual practitioner. This intersection creates something you won't find anywhere else because it can't be institutionalized, sanitized, or made palatable.

This work is my contribution to the spiritual war we're in. The battle for our minds, our sovereignty, our right to exist untamed.

## Being a Survivor

I've survived child sexual abuse, incest, narcissistic abuse, and targeting by those who saw my light and wanted to extinguish it before I learned to wield it. I was diagnosed with Complex PTSD in 2018. In 2020, through collage art therapy and plant medicine, I discovered I'd been living with Dissociative Identity Disorder; my brilliant mind protecting me by splitting into the parts that could survive what I couldn't.

Since then, I've been on a journey of integration, deprogramming, and remembering who I was before they told me who to be.

I know about targeting. I know about gaslighting. I know about institutional betrayal and the ways systems protect abusers while punishing truth-tellers.

I know, because I lived it.

And now I use everything I learned: the law degree, the spiritual gifts, the artistic vision, the hard-won freedom to help other survivors reclaim what's theirs.
